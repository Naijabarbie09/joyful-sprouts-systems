1. **Pause selling until the product is certified.** Both products are active on the store. The Notion compliance ticket (Jul 14) says no certificate exists and Drop 1 cannot legally be sold without one; the Oct 22 launch gate was still open; nothing newer is on record. The 6-12M and 12-18M sleepers also need a sleepwear determination. If the certificate and reports exist somewhere I could not see, this recommendation falls away.
2. **Do not send the Black Friday series as a discount push.** Seven campaigns sit in Klaviyo for Nov 20 to Dec 1. The list is 71 people, email has produced $0, the product is not cleared to sell, and the standing rule is no early deep discounts. A single honest "here is where we are" note is worth more than a five-email sale.
3. **Do not buy traffic yet.** 905 Facebook and Instagram sessions gave 0 add-to-carts, and the average Meta cost per purchase ($39) is higher than either product's price. The Oct 22 paper proposes a $500 to $1,000 ad budget; I would hold it.
4. **Do not commit to 800 swaddles.** The supplier's minimum is 400 per colour. With no sales and declined card payments in October, that order would tie up cash in a product with no proven demand. Source a small quantity for the backers another way, or offer them a substitute.
5. **Settle the bodysuit price before any marketing.** Three prices are on record: $18 (locked May 23), $24 (live), $19 (Oct 22 paper). At $24 it sits at the top of the boutique range; peers are $16 to $25. There is no JSO sales data to settle it, so this is a positioning call. My lean: keep $24 as the single price and sell through bundles, because a $24 item carrying about $9.59 of shipping and tax is a hard first purchase.
6. **Reset the tracker.** 146 of 157 Notion tickets are open and overdue. Working that list is not a plan. Replace it with the stage 0 gate list in `docs/five-year-plan.md`.
7. **On the five-year goal:** it is reachable only with a wider line, registry and wholesale channels, and repeat customers. The current stock is worth $24,720 at full price. I could not verify how long any comparable brand took to reach $1M, so the revenue markers in the plan are targets, not forecasts.
