**Between:** Joyful Sprouts Organics ("JSO") and [Creator name] ("Creator"). **Date:** [date]

**1. Gifted product.** JSO will send Creator the following at no charge: [items and sizes]. [Confirm contents: the 6-12M and 12-18M sleepers are on hold until cleared.] No other payment is owed by either side.

**2. Content.** Creator will post one vertical video of 20 to 30 seconds showing the gifted product, within [7] days of delivery, on [platform]. Creator chooses the creative approach and uses their own words.

**3. Disclosure (required).** Creator will clearly disclose the gift in the caption, near the start, for example "Gifted by Joyful Sprouts Organics", and use the platform's paid partnership or branded content label where available. A disclosure only in hashtags at the end is not enough.

**4. Claims.** Creator will describe only what they personally see, feel and use. Creator will not say the product treats, cures, heals or prevents any condition (including eczema or allergies), and will not use the words "non-toxic", "chemical-free" or "hypoallergenic". Product facts must match JSO's product page. JSO may ask Creator to correct an inaccurate statement. Creator will not use a child's name in the content unless Creator chooses to as a private person.

**5. Rights.** Creator owns the content. Creator grants JSO a non-exclusive license for [6] months from posting to repost it organically on JSO's website, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and emails, with credit to Creator. No paid advertising, whitelisting or boosting without a separate written agreement. After the term JSO stops new uses on request.

**6. Safe use.** Use the product under adult supervision and follow the care label.

**7. Term and changes.** This agreement ends when the content is posted and the license period ends. Changes must be in writing.
