1. Requirements for every product

1.1 Fabric and certification

  • 100% organic cotton. Fabric must be GOTS certified. The manufacturer supplies, before bulk: the supplier's GOTS scope certificate (number, expiry) and the transaction certificate for the fabric lot. The certificate number appears on the commercial invoice.
  • Knit styles (bodysuit, sleeper): interlock, 200 to 220 GSM as produced, minimum 200. Manufacturer states the measured weight of the production fabric in writing.
  • Dyes: AZO-free, GOTS-approved. Pastel colours need approved lab dips (three options per colour) before bulk.
  • Colourfastness to washing, rubbing and perspiration: minimum grade 4 (proposed). Shrinkage after three washes: 5% or less (proposed). Fabric pre-shrunk.
  • Thread and embroidery thread: cotton, colour-matched. No polyester.

1.2 No-substitution rule

Fabric, fibre, hardware, trims, seam type, label content, measurements and packaging are fixed by this pack. If anything cannot be made as written, stop and ask. A change counts only when JSO approves it in writing. The factory states in writing the seam type used on each seam. (Drop 1 flatlock seams were specified and not delivered.)
